Buy – Standard Software
Many companies choose standard software – ready-made solutions developed for a broad target group and offered “off the shelf”.
Examples: Microsoft 365, SAP, Salesforce, Asana, Trello.
Why is it attractive?
• Fast availability – pre-built and quick to deploy
• Proven quality – already in use by many companies (often in the same industry)
• Best practices built in – includes industry-specific know-how, legal compliance, or process
standards
• Customizable – adaptable through configuration (customizing) of forms, workflows, and user roles
